# AVERLYNT — Design Direction

## Thesis
A deliberate route through modular frames: scattered intent becomes a connected system, then a visible outcome. AVERLYNT should feel precise, capable, composed and human—not like a generic AI startup template.

## Palette
- Carbon #0B1115 — primary background
- Deep Slate #17232A — raised surfaces
- Signal Teal #58D6C1 — primary action and progress
- Warm Brass #D8B47A — rare emphasis
- Cloud #F1F4F2 — primary text
- Mist #9BA9A8 — secondary text

## Typography
- Display: Space Grotesk, 600–700
- Body: Inter, 400–600
- Utility: DM Mono, 400–500

## Layout and behavior
Editorial hierarchy, generous whitespace, asymmetric composition, consistent max-width, hairline separators. Responsive from mobile to wide desktop. Visible focus, semantic navigation, keyboard-operable controls and reduced-motion support. Bahasa Indonesia is the default; English is available through a language toggle.

## Content rules
Never invent customers, testimonials, metrics, certifications or performance guarantees. Distinguish current foundations from planned capabilities. Never claim an action exists unless connected and verified.
