# AVERLYNT Product Brief

## Positioning
An independent digital operations platform connecting AI-assisted intelligence, automation, focused software and digital commerce into practical systems.

## Initial audience
Indonesian founders, small-business owners and independent operators who need leverage and repeatable systems without building a large team.

## Promise
Turn intent into working systems that move work forward.

## Public website goal
Explain the platform direction, communicate the connected capability model and invite visitors to explore. This first release is a brand and product-direction site, not a claim that every capability is already live.

## Principles
- Start with a real business need, not a tool list.
- Build capability in validated increments.
- Keep human oversight and clear control around automation.
- Avoid vendor lock-in where practical.
- Rp0-first infrastructure; no paid service, subscription, prepayment or billing activation without explicit owner approval.

## Capability map
- Intelligence: research, opportunity understanding, planning and decision support.
- Automation: repeatable workflows and agent-assisted execution.
- Software: focused tools and workspaces.
- Commerce: productization, distribution and digital revenue operations.

## Status
Brand and public website foundation. Product capabilities remain subject to validation and phased delivery.
